# Current Project

## 🥗 CuchiNutri

CuchiNutri is an AI-powered nutrition platform designed to become a personalized nutrition assistant.

Instead of offering generic diet plans, CuchiNutri understands each user's unique context and generates personalized recommendations based on:

- Physical goals
- Lifestyle
- Habits
- Budget
- Food preferences
- Supermarket availability

### Vision

Imagine having an intelligent nutritionist that:

- knows your objectives
- knows your body
- knows your routine
- knows your favourite supermarket
- creates your shopping list automatically
- continuously adapts your nutrition

That's the future I'm building.
